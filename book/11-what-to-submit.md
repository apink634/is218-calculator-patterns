# What to submit

Submit the URL of **your completed repository** in your Canvas assignment. Do not submit the instructor repository, your GitHub profile, or only an Actions URL. Keep the book files in your fork. Create `STUDENT_README.md` for your own setup instructions and design explanations. Put the successful Actions run link there. Push your work before submitting and make sure the instructor can access your repository.
