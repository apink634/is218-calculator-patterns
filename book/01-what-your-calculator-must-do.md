# What your calculator must do

* Read commands such as `add 5 3`, `subtract 5 3`, `multiply 5 3`, and `divide 6 3`.
* Show the answer and keep accepting commands until the user types `exit`.
* Support `history` to show successful calculations in order. Do not save failed calculations.
* Show a helpful message for an unknown command, missing or extra operands, invalid numbers, and division by zero. Keep running after these errors.
* Accept decimals and negative numbers. End cleanly on end-of-input (EOF).

Example: `add 5 3` prints `8` or `8.0`. Either format is fine. No GUI, database, plugins, or undo feature is required.
